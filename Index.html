The Land

A Living Tropical Garden

The property is more than a house. It is surrounded by an established tropical environment with fruit trees, plants, greenhouses and abundant wildlife.

The land includes jackfruit, Surinam cherry, sugar apple, cacao, coffee, bananas, plantains and herbs.

Wildlife in the surrounding area can include monkeys, sloths, toucans, scarlet macaws, butterflies, coatis and many other tropical birds.
